✦ Safety & Guidelines

We'd rather rehabilitate you than remove you.

Rules only matter if people understand why they exist. Here's the actual system — not just a wall of "don'ts."

Our biggest departure from most servers

A non-ban policy, genuinely

Bangwing IN prioritizes rehabilitation over removal. Instead of banning people outright, we lean on permanent mutes, a real appeal process, and graduated responses that scale with severity and intent.

Intent is actually checked before consequences are decided: an honest mistake gets education, not punishment. Recklessness or malice gets treated a lot more seriously. Moderation is human-led throughout, with cultural context factored in — not a bot issuing verdicts.

If something feels unfair

Here's exactly how to report it

01 · Fastest

Type $ticket anywhere

Opens a private channel with admins, instantly — no need to find the right person first.

02 · Direct

Tag @mod or @help

Gets you direct assistance from staff or the wider community fast.

03 · Formal

File it in Complaint Box

Include what happened, who was involved, when, and any evidence. Your identity stays confidential by default.

Nothing gets resolved in public call-outs — that's by design. Staff can't let bias or personal feelings influence a decision, every case is evidence-based, and if a complaint doesn't go your way, there's a real escalation path up to senior staff and, if needed, bangwings@zohomail.in directly.

The rulebook, department by department

Eight departments, in plain language

The full legal-style wording lives in-server. Here's what each one actually means for you.

  • Share real opinions without fear of retaliation — as long as it doesn't degrade anyone for who they are.
  • Satire has to actually read as satire. "It was a joke" doesn't undo harm after the fact.
  • No dangerous misinformation — fake health cures, conspiracy presented as fact, doxxing information.
  • Criticize decisions and actions, not someone's character or identity.
  • You're always told why a moderation action was taken against you.
  • Post your own work, or get permission and credit the original creator.
  • Everything needs to work for a community that includes members aged 13 and up.
  • No reaction-begging, mass-tagging, or manipulating engagement for visibility.
  • Zero exceptions, no gray area: nothing sexual involving minors, no hate symbols, nothing glorifying self-harm.
  • A stated boundary is final. Pushing past it — even by guilt-tripping — counts as harassment.
  • Before jumping to solutions: "do you want advice, or just someone to listen?"
  • No backseat moderating — report an issue to staff instead of publicly calling someone out.
  • Real apologies name the specific action, its impact, and what changes. Not just "sorry."
  • Respect whoever's DJing — don't skip or disrupt songs you didn't queue.
  • No phishing links, IP grabbers, or "free Nitro" scams. Ever. Ask staff to verify if unsure.
  • Give other people room — don't monopolize an event or a discussion.
  • Be patient with time zones and with members still learning English.
  • This department governs staff behavior, not member behavior.
  • Personal feelings or bias are not allowed to influence enforcement decisions.
  • Your privacy is protected by default the moment you file a report.
  • Decisions are evidence-based — timestamps, screenshots and specifics genuinely help your case.
  • Reporting something never puts your identity at risk. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a serious violation.
  • "They started it" does not excuse your own violations.
  • Intent genuinely changes the outcome: accidents get education, malice gets serious consequences.
  • Found a loophole? Report it through Feedback — exploiting it is treated as its own violation.
  • You can't judge the whole server off one bad interaction with one person.
  • Different humor, energy levels, and communication styles are all genuinely welcome.
  • Discomfort gets managed through blocking and channel choice — not by demanding someone else be excluded.
  • Age, religion, nationality, and language are never grounds for exclusion.
  • Community-first, transparently: funding goes back into the server, not out of it.
  • No pay-to-win systems, no data exploitation, no paid moderation roles.
  • Membership unlocks content access — not a competitive advantage over anyone else.

Age-appropriate by design

13+ generally. 18+ spaces are actually gated.

General and public channels are kept family-friendly and safe for our 13+ community. Anything more mature sits behind genuine 18+ age verification, in its own separate space — not just a warning label on the same channel everyone else uses.

Mental health & well-being

You're allowed to not be okay here

Members can seek support openly — content glorifying self-harm or suicide isn't permitted, but talking about struggling with either is never treated as a violation. There are designated vent channels for heavier emotional content, and late-night voice chats exist for exactly the kind of conversation that helps at 1am.

If you're going through something difficult, the community can be a genuine support — but it isn't a substitute for professional help when you need it.

Now you know the rules. Come use the server anyway.
