First few minutes

What to expect when you join

Step 1

A real welcome, not a bot message

You'll land on our guide pages first — quick rules, a support forum, social links, and a rundown of every channel and what it's for.

Step 2

Pick your interest roles

Ten categories to choose from — Content Creation, Reading & Writing, Cooking, Fitness, Singing & Music, Gaming & Adventure, Photography & Videography, Art & Craft, Coding & Tech, Travel & Exploration — plus 20+ specific gaming roles. Roles are mentionable, so you can ping your way to people who share them.

Step 3

Say "Namaste" in main chat

It's a small tradition here — say it, and you'll get a proper welcome back. Not required, but it's a good icebreaker.

Step 4

Post one thing, anywhere

A photo in Gallery, a thought in Focus, or just a hello in General. That's genuinely most of what it takes to stop being "new."
