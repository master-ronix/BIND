Music

Background radio, or full control — your choice

Sangeet Radio

Our own bot runs a 24/7 queue on the Spotlight stage, shaped by what the community actually listens to. No manual DJ-ing required.

Music 1 & 2

Two dedicated voice channels for manual playback if you want to run your own queue with friends.

Playlist channel

Drop links from Spotify, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, Gaana or almost any platform — each one gets its own discussion thread.

Voice & stage

Spotlight, and a room of your own

  • Spotlight — the main event stage. Leveled members and rank holders host debates, live singing, movie and gameplay streams, or just open conversation.
  • Six stage rooms — Chill, Hangout, Melody, Theatre, Streaming, and Gaming, each built for a different kind of hangout, not one generic voice channel trying to do everything.
  • Session — join it and it instantly generates a private voice channel named after you, with full control from a dashboard: lock it, rename it, set a limit, keep it friends-only.

Games

Low-stakes, high-replay mini-games

  • Gamezone — a rotating set of bot-run mini-games for whenever chat needs a break.
  • Numpy — number-sequence games that quietly turn into math practice.
  • Wordpad — word-association and vocabulary games that get more chaotic than you'd expect.

✦ Where it all started

Our Minecraft SMP is live

Bangwing IN began as a Minecraft SMP group before it was ever a Discord server (read the full story on Our Story) — the world is still up and connected straight into the server through an in-game chat relay.

bangwings.play.hosting

Java Edition · paste the address directly into "Add Server"

Beacon

A world-news channel that auto-threads live headlines from trusted global sources, updated continuously — so you can stay informed without leaving the server.

Confession & Starboard

Post anonymously in Confession — genuinely anonymous, even server owners can't trace a post back to you unless misuse triggers a logged review — or get featured on Starboard when the community can't stop reacting to what you posted.

Casting

Members with the Creator role get their live streams and YouTube uploads automatically featured here — free promotion, no algorithm required.

Not off-the-shelf

Three bots, built by us, not bought

Bangwing IN has deliberately cut down on third-party bots in favor of systems built specifically for this community.

  • Bard — reads your live activity and assigns roles accordingly: streaming gets you "streaming," playing a game gets "playing," Spotify gets "vibing," watching something gets "chilling" — even a specific game like Valorant gets its own live role.
  • Nyla — text moderation with a tiered "heat" system that decays over time, so a bad moment doesn't follow you around forever.
  • Sangeet — the radio bot behind 24/7 music on the Spotlight stage.
