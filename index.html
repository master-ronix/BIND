What that actually means

Three words get thrown around a lot. Here's what they mean here.

Every Discord server calls itself a "community." Ours is built around three specific, deliberate things — not slogans.

Social community

A place, not a pitch

A space to share laughter, hobbies and interests — to skill up alongside people who care, express yourself across different forums, and build bonds that hold up outside the app too.

Healthy interaction

Non-toxic, on purpose

Security, shared trust, and the room to just be yourself. Not "purer than everywhere else" — just a team that takes unfair treatment seriously and actually does something about it.

Quality engagement

Activity forums, not feeds

The heart of the server. Dedicated spaces for art, photography, writing, tech and more — built to feel like a real creative outlet, not another timeline to scroll.

A note from the founder

"I started this because I was tired of the alternative."

"There are a lot of servers out there promising you a family and delivering a sales funnel — giveaways to keep you scrolling, fake urgency to make you join, drama dressed up as community. Bangwing IN doesn't have any of that. It has activity forums, a few hundred people who actually talk to each other, and a team that will genuinely go to bat for you if something feels unfair. That's it. That's the whole pitch."

— Rachit Ranjan, Founder
