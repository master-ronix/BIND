What kinds of events happen here

The full range, honestly

  • Creative challenges — art contests, writing challenges, photography competitions
  • Talent shows & radio jams — member showcases and music sessions on the Spotlight stage
  • Parliament sessions — structured community discussions, run regularly
  • Art books & themed writing challenges — collaborative, structured literary projects
  • Gaming tournaments & game nights — multiplayer competitions and casual sessions
  • Community celebrations — cultural festivals and milestone events
  • Streams & voice hangouts — scheduled and spontaneous
  • Giveaways — yes, sometimes — but they're not what we lead with

Getting involved

You don't have to compete to matter here

Events are announced in #Broadcast first, with a rough calendar kept by the staff team. Got an idea for one? Drop it in the Feedback Box — plenty of past events started exactly that way.

And if competing isn't your thing: come as an audience. Genuinely — that's most of what makes these events feel alive in the first place.

The next one's coming. Be in the room for it.
